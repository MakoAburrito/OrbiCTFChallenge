CTF FAQ

What is a CTF?

CTF stands for Capture the Flag. In this mini CTF, you'll solve a series of cybersecurity-themed challenges and look for hidden flags that look like PLUTO{...}.

Do I need cybersecurity experience?

Nope! Operation ORBI is beginner-friendly. Each challenge includes optional hints if you get stuck.

How do I earn a raffle ticket?

Complete Operation ORBI and show your completion screen to a PlutoHacks organizer wearing an orange organizer shirt. They will verify your completion and give you your raffle ticket.

Where can I get help?

Ask any PlutoHacks organizer in an orange shirt or visit the registration/help desk. We can give you a nudge without spoiling the challenge.

Can I use Google, DevTools, Python, or other tools?

Yes! Research and experimentation are part of the CTF. You may use your browser's developer tools, scripts, command-line tools, or other resources to investigate the challenges.

Can I work with someone else?

You can discuss ideas and learn together, but try solving the challenges yourself so you get the full experience.

What if I get completely stuck?

Start with the hints on the challenge page. If you still need help, ask an organizer. Getting a hint does not disqualify you from completing the CTF.

Do I need to install anything?

Most challenges can be solved with a browser and free tools. Some challenges may be easier with tools such as image metadata viewers, Python, or browser developer tools.
